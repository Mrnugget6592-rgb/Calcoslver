Neon War

By OnlineGames.io

About Neon War

Neon War is a free online arcade shooter developed by Qky Games where your job is to blast glowing neon shapes as they pop out of black holes scattered across the screen. Each shape carries a number showing how many hits it takes to destroy, and your golden cannon fires automatically once it's aimed — all you have to do is steer it. Precision matters more than speed here, since letting shapes bounce around too long puts your cannon's health bar at risk.

Playable free in your browser with no download required, Neon War works on both desktop and mobile devices. Its bright, minimalist visuals and simple horizontal-aiming control scheme make it an easy pick-up-and-play arcade game, while the upgrade system built around blue hexagons gives it a light progression loop that keeps runs feeling fresh.

Gold Battery and Shape Numbers

The gold battery icon in the upper-left corner of the screen tracks your cannon's health, which drops any time a bouncing neon shape touches it. Since the shapes keep bouncing along the platform until destroyed, avoiding contact with them is just as important as hitting them — each shape displays a number indicating exactly how many hits it needs before it's cleared, so prioritize the low-number shapes closest to your cannon first.

Upgrading with Blue Hexagons

Destroying shapes drops blue hexagons, which you spend to upgrade your cannon. Each upgrade adds roughly +5% power to its category:

  • Fire Rate — shoot faster to clear blocks from the platform more quickly.
  • Damage — increase the harm each shot deals to shapes.
  • Health — raise your battery's total health for longer runs.
  • Pet — unlock a companion for your cannon that fires alongside you.

Tips for Winning at Neon War

Focus your aim on shapes with the lowest numbers first to clear threats quickly, and never let more than one or two shapes bounce near your cannon at once. Invest early upgrade hexagons into Fire Rate to snowball your clearing speed, then balance in Damage and Health as the shapes get tougher. Keeping the cannon centered between waves gives you the fastest reaction time when new shapes emerge from the black holes.

How to Play Neon War

  1. Watch the black holes — neon shapes with hit-count numbers emerge from them.
  2. Aim your cannon — click and drag left or right to steer.
  3. Let it auto-fire — your cannon shoots continuously once aimed.
  4. Avoid contact — dodge bouncing shapes to protect your gold battery.
  5. Collect blue hexagons — earned by destroying shapes.
  6. Upgrade your cannon — spend hexagons on Fire Rate, Damage, Health, or Pet.
  7. Survive and score — clear as many shapes as possible before your health runs out.
